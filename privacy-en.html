YUTAKANO ("we", "us" or "the Operator") has set out this Privacy Policy ("this Policy") to explain how user information is handled in "Calfect" ("the App"), an app for smartphones and tablets.

Summary: The App has no sign-up, and we do not run servers to collect your information. Your events, photos, location and settings are handled only on your device. Information leaves your device only in the situations described in this Policy, such as showing ads, checking your paid plan, checking for app updates, getting weather forecasts, loading calendars from URLs you added, and sending feedback from the Suggestion box.

1. Information handled only on your device

The following information is used on your device to make the App work. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

Calendars and events

With your permission, the App reads the events in the calendars on your device (iCloud, Google, Outlook and so on) and shows, adds, edits and deletes them. Event details (title, date and time, location, notes and so on) are processed only on your device.

If you sync your calendars with a cloud service, the sync takes place between your device and that service, and your information is managed under that provider's privacy policy.

Photos and camera

When you attach photos to an event, the App receives the photos you choose or take. Photos are stored only in the App's storage on your device and are not sent anywhere. The links between photos and events are also managed only on your device.

Location (optional)

Only if you turn on "Use your location for weather" in Settings, and with your permission, the App gets your device's approximate location while you are using the App. Your location is used only to choose the place for the weather forecast.

  • When you are in Japan, the latitude and longitude themselves are not stored. Only the code of the chosen area (such as a prefecture) is stored on your device. Your latitude and longitude are not sent to us or to the weather providers.
  • When you are outside Japan, the App stores on your device a point rounded to one decimal place (about 10 km) and its place name (for example, "Seattle, Washington"), and sends only the rounded point to the forecast provider (see "Weather forecasts" in Section 2). Your exact latitude and longitude before rounding are never stored or sent.
  • To find the prefecture or place name for your location, the App uses the address lookup built into your device's operating system (provided by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and by Google on Android). These lookups are handled under each company's privacy policy.

While this feature is off, the App does not get your location.

Instead of using your location, you can also search for a city outside Japan by name. In that case, the city name you enter is passed to your operating system's place search (Apple or Google, as above), and the rounded point and name of the city you choose are stored on your device.

App settings

Your settings, such as display options, colors, stamps, calendar URLs you added, language and app icon choice, and your guardian records (bonds, guardians you raised, the number of special omikuji draws and so on) are stored on your device. The backup feature puts your settings and photos into a single file only when you use it, and saves it to the location you choose. On iPhone and iPad, if you turn on daily automatic backup to iCloud Drive, the same file is saved once a day to your own iCloud Drive (handled under Apple's privacy policy). We never receive your backups.

2. When the App communicates outside your device

The App communicates with outside services in the situations below. As part of any connection, the other party receives information such as your IP address and the time of access.

Weather forecasts

If you turn on weather forecasts in Settings, the App gets forecast data, and observed temperatures (AMeDAS) used to compare with the previous day, from the website of the Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA). The request includes only the codes of the area or observation station shown. It does not include information that identifies you or the latitude and longitude of your location.

If you open the rain radar from the weather screen, the App gets rain images from JMA's website and map images from the website of the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI). The request includes only the map area shown (the tile numbers). It does not include information that identifies you or the latitude and longitude of your location.

For places in Japan, on iPhone and iPad, the App gets forecasts from Apple's WeatherKit (Apple Weather) for hourly forecasts, days beyond JMA's weekly forecast, and wind forecasts beyond the day after tomorrow. The request includes only a representative point of the forecast area (one fixed latitude and longitude per area). It does not include your location or information that identifies you.

For places outside Japan, the App gets forecasts from Apple's WeatherKit on iPhone and iPad, and from the forecast service of MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) on Android. The request includes only the latitude and longitude of a point rounded to about 10 km (made from your location or from the city you chose). It does not include information that identifies you. As MET Norway's terms require, requests to MET Norway identify the App by its name and the Operator's website URL. Outside Japan, the rain radar and typhoon information are not shown, and no communication is made for them.

Calendars added by URL

Calendars whose URLs you add are loaded regularly from the publisher of that URL. The App does not send your events or any other personal information to the publisher. What the publisher records depends on its own rules. Please add only URLs from publishers you trust.

Map apps

When you tap the Map button on an event, the address or place entered in the event is passed to the map app you chose (Google Maps or your device's built-in map app). The information is handled under that map app provider's privacy policy.

Suggestion box

Only when you tap Send in the Suggestion box in Settings, the type you chose, the text you entered and your device type and OS version (for example, "ios 26.0") are sent to us through Google Forms, a service of Google LLC. Information that identifies you, such as your name or email address, is not sent automatically. Please do not include personal information such as your name or contact details in your feedback.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, an ad service of Google LLC. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, information such as your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android), IP address, device model, OS and language, and the history of ads shown and tapped is sent to Google.

To show more suitable ads, the App may also show ads from Unity Ads and the LINE Ad Network through Google AdMob's mediation. When these ads are selected or shown, the same kinds of information as above are also sent to each ad provider.

If you choose "Watch an ad to try free" on the Calfect Premium screen, the App shows a video or other ad (rewarded ad). This ad is delivered in the same way as above.

Paid plan management (RevenueCat)

The App uses a service of RevenueCat, Inc. to check your Calfect Premium status and restore purchases. An anonymous user ID created automatically in the App, your purchase history and information such as your device model and OS are sent.

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive payment information such as credit card numbers.

App update checks (Expo)

To deliver fixes quickly, the App uses EAS Update, an update delivery service of 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo). When the App starts, it asks Expo's servers whether a new update is available and downloads it if there is one. The App's version and update number, the OS type, and an identifier created in the App (which does not identify you or your device) are sent. Your events, photos, settings and other content are not sent.

4. Ad tracking and consent

On iPhone and iPad, the App asks the first time you open it whether you allow tracking using the advertising identifier. If you do not allow it, you can still use the App as usual, and ads that are not tailored to you are shown. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

For users in the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions, the App asks for consent for ads as required by law. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" on the Calfect Premium screen in the App.

On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

While you use the paid plan (Calfect Premium), including while trying it by watching an ad, the App shows no ads and sends no information to Google AdMob or the mediation ad providers.

5. Information we do not collect

  • The App has no sign-up or login, and we never collect your name, email address, phone number and so on.
  • The App does not include any analytics service or any service that collects usage logs or crash logs.
  • The App does not read your device's contacts (address book).

6. Information you give us with feedback or inquiries

If you send feedback from the Suggestion box, we receive the type you chose, the text you entered and your device type and OS version. We use these only to improve the App and delete them when they are no longer needed. The Suggestion box only receives messages; we do not reply to it.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your inquiry (including any images attached). We use these only to reply to you and improve the App, and delete them after the period needed to handle your inquiry.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Settings and photos stored on your device are deleted when you delete them in the App or delete (uninstall) the App from your device. Events in your device's calendars remain even if you delete the App.
  • You can revoke access to your location and calendars at any time in your device's Settings app.
  • How long information sent to the third parties listed in Sections 2 and 3 is kept depends on each provider's rules.

8. Sharing with third parties

Except for the transmissions described in this Policy and where required by law, we do not provide your information to third parties. We never sell your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on the laws where you live, you may ask us to disclose, correct, delete or stop using the information we hold about you. The only information we hold about you is the feedback and inquiries described in Section 6. If you wish to make a request, please contact us at the address at the end of this Policy.

For information held by third-party services, please contact each provider listed in the table in Section 3.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK

We are the controller of the information handling described in this Policy. We handle information on the basis of providing the App (performance of a contract), improving the App and preventing fraud (legitimate interests), and your consent (for example, for ads tailored to you). You can withdraw your consent at any time as described in Section 4. In addition to the rights above, you may request data portability or object to the handling of your information, and you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country.

Users in California and other US states

We do not sell your personal information for money. When ads are shown, information such as your advertising identifier may be sent to the ad providers listed in Section 2, which may count as "sharing" under some state laws. You can stop ads tailored to you as described in Section 4 (the tracking setting on iPhone and iPad, deleting your advertising ID on Android, and so on). While you use the paid plan, no ad-related information is sent. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

10. Handling of information outside your country

We are based in Japan, and the App is also offered in countries and regions outside Japan. Because the App uses third-party services such as Google AdMob (including the mediation ad providers), RevenueCat, Expo, Apple (WeatherKit) and MET Norway, information sent may be processed on servers outside the country where you live, including in the United States and Norway. It is handled under each provider's privacy policy.

11. Children

The App is not directed mainly at children under 13.
